Escalation Timing and Acknowledgment SLA Guide (Read-Only Reference)

Purpose
- Define time-bound escalation and acknowledgment expectations for operational bulletins.
- Ensure consistent response behavior across teams and severities.
- Support measurable service levels with clear accountability.

What this tool does
- Returns a static guide for escalation timing and acknowledgment SLAs.
- Does not page responders or monitor live acknowledgments.
- Does not mutate on-call schedules.

Invocation
- Call with no arguments.
- Output is a single Markdown string.

SLA principles
- deadlines must be explicit and timezone-safe.
- severity determines required acknowledgment speed.
- escalation must be deterministic, not discretionary.
- missed SLAs trigger follow-up and reporting.

Severity tiers
- Sev-0: critical production outage or safety-impacting event.
- Sev-1: major degradation with high business impact.
- Sev-2: moderate impact requiring planned response.
- Sev-3: low impact informational event.

Acknowledgment targets
- Sev-0: acknowledge within 5 minutes.
- Sev-1: acknowledge within 15 minutes.
- Sev-2: acknowledge within 60 minutes.
- Sev-3: acknowledge within 1 business day.

Escalation ladder
1) primary steward bulletin.
2) backup steward bulletin.
3) team on-call lead.
4) incident commander or duty manager.
5) executive duty channel for prolonged critical impact.

Escalation timing defaults
- Sev-0: escalate every 5 minutes until acknowledged.
- Sev-1: escalate every 15 minutes.
- Sev-2: escalate every 60 minutes.
- Sev-3: no automatic ladder unless policy override exists.

Acknowledgment semantics
- acknowledgment means responder confirmed stewardship of next action.
- read receipts alone are not acknowledgment.
- automation acknowledgments require service account labeling.
- handoff must preserve stewardship continuity.

Tracking payload
{
  "event_id": "string",
  "severity": "sev0|sev1|sev2|sev3",
  "first_sent_utc": "string",
  "ack_deadline_utc": "string",
  "ack_received_utc": "string|null",
  "current_escalation_level": 0,
  "steward": "string|null"
}

SLA breach handling
- flag breach in incident timeline.
- notify escalation manager automatically.
- capture root cause category for missed acknowledgment.
- create corrective action item with steward and due date.

Timezone and calendar rules
- store and evaluate all deadlines in UTC.
- optionally display local time for recipients.
- document business-hours exceptions explicitly.
- avoid ambiguous terms like "end of day".

Operational controls
- test escalation pipelines weekly.
- validate contact freshness for escalation chain.
- protect against duplicate pages from parallel systems.
- simulate missed-ack scenarios in drills.

Common anti-patterns
- severity inflation to force fast responses.
- unclear stewardship after first acknowledgment.
- silent suppression of escalation events.
- inconsistent SLA definitions across tools.

Metrics
- acknowledgment latency by severity.
- breach rate by team and channel.
- escalation depth before acknowledgment.
- false escalation rate.
- mean time to assign accountable steward.
